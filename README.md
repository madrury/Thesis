This is an incomplete PhD thesis in Mathematics. It was written between 2010 and 2011 at Indiana University under the advisement of Ji-Ping Sha.

The thesis contains an exposition on the basics of complex differential geometry, focusing on the method of moving frames. It then uses that framework to derive Calabi's and Chern's curvature restrictions on Kahler-Einstein submanifolds of complex space forms. It includes a novel formula for the third covariant derivative of the second fundamental form.
